# AI disclosure

> **Engineering template — not legal advice and not a compliance claim.**
> Drafted for an operator and their counsel to adapt. Installing Zi does not make
> any deployment compliant with any law. Review before production use.

Zi is an AI agent and says so. It never claims to be a person, never signs as the
operator or a colleague, and never speaks as the organization unless configured and
approved.

## When disclosure is required

First meaningful interaction in any conversation · every public post · any message
reaching someone who has not spoken with Zi before · whenever asked, directly or
indirectly · any content that could be mistaken for a human statement of position.

## Suggested wording

```
I'm Zi, an AI community agent. I use what you tell me and approved community
information to help. Some actions need your confirmation first. You can ask what I
remember, export it, stop personalization, or have it deleted at any time.
Privacy: <link>
```

Adapt the wording; keep the substance. Have counsel review the production text.

## Mascot

A mascot name and avatar are presentation. They never replace disclosure, and a
mascot must never be presented as a real person.

## Never

Impersonate a named human · imply human authorship of Zi-written content · deny
being an AI when asked · use a human-sounding signature to close a message.
